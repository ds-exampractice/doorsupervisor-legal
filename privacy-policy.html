Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 9, 2026

This policy applies to the Door Supervisor Exam Practice Android app and this website (doorsupervisorexam.co.uk).

1. Introduction

SAABB Ventures (Pvt) Ltd ("we", "our", "us") operates the Door Supervisor Exam Practice mobile application. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information (when you create an account)

  • Email address (required for account creation)
  • Name (optional, for personalisation)
  • Study progress and performance data
  • Device information (type, OS version)

Usage Information (collected automatically)

  • App session data (features used, time spent)
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Anonymised analytics data

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the Service
  • Track your study progress and performance
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Send important service notifications
  • Provide customer support
  • Analyse usage patterns to improve the app

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

Service Providers

  • Firebase (Google) — app backend and analytics
  • RevenueCat — payment processing
  • Google Play Console — app distribution and crash reporting

Legal

We may disclose data if required by law or in response to a valid legal request.

5. Data Security

  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security assessments
  • Access limited on a need-to-know basis
No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We use industry-standard measures but cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

  • Access — request copies of your personal data
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion (submit a request)
  • Portability — transfer your data to another service
  • Objection — object to how we process your data

To exercise any right, email info@doorsupervisorexam.co.uk.

7. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained while your account is active
  • Study progress: retained to maintain your learning history
  • Anonymised analytics: may be retained indefinitely
  • Support communications: retained for 2 years after resolution

8. Children's Privacy

Our Service is for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. Contact us immediately if you believe a minor has provided their data.

9. International Transfers

Data may be processed outside the UK. All transfers comply with UK GDPR and appropriate safeguards are in place.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date on this page
  • Post the updated policy here
  • Notify you through the app for significant changes
