Data Deletion Request

Last Updated: March 9, 2026

⚠️ Warning: Once your data is deleted, this action cannot be undone. You will permanently lose all study progress, performance history and account information.

1. Your Right to Data Deletion

We respect your right to control your personal data under UK GDPR. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

2. What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Account information (email, name, profile data)
  • Study progress and performance history
  • App usage statistics and analytics data
  • Device and session information
  • Support communications (after 30 days)

3. Data That May Be Retained (anonymised)

  • Aggregated usage statistics (no personal identifiers)
  • Financial transaction records (required by law)
  • Security logs (anonymised after 12 months)

4. Deletion Timeline

5. Submit Your Deletion Request

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your data, send us an email from the address associated with your account. Include confirmation that you want all data permanently deleted.

Or send by email
📧 Send Deletion Request by Email

Clicking the button opens your email app with a pre-filled request. Send it from the email address linked to your account. We will process your request within 72 hours.

Alternative: Email info@doorsupervisorexam.co.uk directly with subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your account email address.

6. Data Protection Rights

Beyond deletion, you also have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Portability — receive your data in a transferable format
  • Objection — object to how we process your data

To exercise these rights: info@doorsupervisorexam.co.uk
